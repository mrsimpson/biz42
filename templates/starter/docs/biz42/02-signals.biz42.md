# Signals

<!--
biz42 chapter 2. Documents observable external and internal conditions that may
affect the business. Signals are impersonal — they exist whether or not anyone
is watching. They are the raw input from which risks and opportunities are derived.

ISO 9001:2015 mapping: Clause 4.1 — Understanding the organization and its context.

A signal is NOT an opinion or a strategic choice — it is an observable fact or trend.
"AI adoption is accelerating in our customer segment" is a signal.
"We should invest in AI" is a response to a signal, not a signal itself.

For each signal, write a ## section with a prose paragraph describing what you observe,
where you observe it, and why it matters to this organizational unit. Tag it as
external or internal.

External signals come from outside the organization: market trends, regulatory changes,
technology shifts, competitive moves, macro-economic conditions.

Internal signals come from within: employee attrition patterns, technical debt
accumulation, capability gaps, cultural shifts, operational metrics.

Each signal should reference the risks and/or opportunities it gives rise to
(see 04-risks.biz42.md and 05-opportunities.biz42.md). A signal that gives rise
to neither risk nor opportunity may be irrelevant to the current scope.

Order signals by impact — most significant first.

Example:

## EU AI Act Enforcement Timeline

The EU AI Act's provisions for high-risk AI systems take effect in August 2026.
Several of our workflow automation features use ML-based decision support that
likely falls under the high-risk category, requiring conformity assessments
and human oversight mechanisms.

Source: external (regulatory).
Gives rise to: risk-ai-act-compliance, opportunity-compliance-as-feature.

## Rising Customer Acquisition Cost

Our blended CAC has increased 35% year-over-year while conversion rates are flat.
Sales cycle length in the mid-market segment has grown from 45 to 68 days.

Source: internal (sales metrics).
Gives rise to: risk-unit-economics, opportunity-self-serve-onboarding.

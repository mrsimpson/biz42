Unlike the other chapters, Evaluation is not a list of entities — it is a described
practice. It answers: what do we review, how often, and who is in the room?

This chapter should cover:

11.1 Review Cadence
  How often does the leadership team review the measures defined in chapter 7?
  Monthly? Quarterly? What triggers an ad-hoc review?

11.2 Review Participants
  Who participates in each review cycle? Which owners (chapter 8) are present?
  Who has authority to escalate findings?

11.3 Escalation Criteria
  What measure thresholds or patterns trigger escalation? When does a failing
  measure become an improvement action (chapter 12)?

The evaluation practice is what keeps the model alive. Without it, objectives
and measures are written once and forgotten. ISO 9001 auditors specifically check
that management review happens and produces evidence of follow-through.

Example:

## 11.1 Review Cadence

Monthly business review on the first Monday of each month. All measures from
chapter 7 are reviewed. Quarterly deep-dive includes capability gap analysis
(chapter 9) and risk re-assessment (chapter 4).

Ad-hoc review is triggered when any measure hits its failure threshold.

## 11.2 Review Participants

Monthly: CEO, CTO, VP Growth, Head of Platform, Finance lead.
Quarterly: above plus all capability owners and product owners.

## 11.3 Escalation Criteria

Any measure in failure state for two consecutive months is escalated to
an improvement action (chapter 12). Any new risk assessed as high impact
is escalated immediately to the next monthly review.

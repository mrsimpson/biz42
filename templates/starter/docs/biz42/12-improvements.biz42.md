# Improvements

<!--
biz42 chapter 12. Documents what the business changes when evaluation reveals a gap,
a failure, or a better way. This chapter closes the loop — improvements feed back
into the model by creating new signals, modifying capabilities, changing products,
or revising objectives.

ISO 9001:2015 mapping: Clause 10 — Improvement (nonconformity, corrective action,
continual improvement).

There are two kinds of improvements:

Corrective actions — fixing what went wrong. A measure failed, a risk materialized,
an expectation was not met. The corrective action addresses the root cause, not
just the symptom.

Continual improvements — making things better even when they're not broken. A
capability can be strengthened, a product can be enhanced, a process can be
streamlined. These are proactive, not reactive.

For each improvement, write a ## section describing what changed, why, and what
it affects in the model.

Each improvement should reference:
- The evaluation finding that triggered it (chapter 11)
- The target: which capability, product, objective, or other element is being changed
- The expected outcome

Improvements may:
- Create new signals (internal) → update chapter 2
- Modify capabilities → update chapter 9
- Change products & services → update chapter 10
- Revise objectives → update chapter 6
- Introduce new risks or opportunities → update chapters 4 and 5

This is where the model proves it is alive. A biz42 model with an empty
improvements chapter has never been reviewed — or its reviews have no teeth.

Example:

## Self-Serve Onboarding Initiative

Triggered by: measure-cac exceeding failure threshold for two consecutive months
(see evaluation chapter 11).

Action: fund and staff a product-led growth squad to build self-serve onboarding.
Establish capability-product-led-growth (currently a gap in chapter 9). Target:
reduce median onboarding time from 22 days to 7 days.

Affects:
- capability-product-led-growth: status changes from "gap" to "planned"
- product-platform: new onboarding feature set
- product-professional-services: shifts from mandatory to optional

Expected outcome: CAC reduction of 25% by Q4 2026, aligning with objective
obj-unit-economics.

## Pipeline Knowledge Transfer

Triggered by: risk-pipeline-dependency assessed as high in quarterly review.

Action: pair programming rotation — every engineer spends one sprint per quarter
working on the data pipeline. Document pipeline architecture in arc42.

Affects:
- capability-data-processing: status remains "existing" but fragility is reduced
- risk-pipeline-dependency: status changes from "open" to "mitigated"

Expected outcome: pipeline incident resolution no longer depends on a single person.

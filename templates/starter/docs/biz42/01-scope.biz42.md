<!--
biz42 chapter 1. Defines what this organizational unit covers and what it explicitly
does not. Every other chapter in this model is bounded by scope — signals, expectations,
capabilities, and products are only relevant if they fall within it.

ISO 9001:2015 mapping: Clause 4.3 — Determining the scope of the management system.

A scope statement has three parts:

1.1 Included
  What markets, geographies, customer segments, product categories, and business
  activities are in scope. Be specific — "enterprise software" is vague,
  "B2B workflow automation for European mid-market companies" is a scope.

1.2 Excluded
  What is deliberately out. Exclusions prevent scope creep and make trade-offs
  visible. Every exclusion should have a rationale — not "because we said so"
  but "because it conflicts with our core capability" or "because the regulatory
  cost is disproportionate."

1.3 Boundaries
  Where this unit's scope meets other organizational units' scopes. If another
  division covers a market you don't, name it. If a shared-services team provides
  a capability you consume, reference it. Boundaries become expectations
  (see 03-expectations.biz42.md).

When this model describes a sub-unit (department, team, product line), its scope
must fit within its parent unit's scope. A mismatch is a finding.

Example:

## 1.1 Included

ACME Corp's B2B SaaS division covers workflow automation products for mid-market
companies (200–2000 employees) in the EU and UK. This includes the core platform,
integrations marketplace, and professional services for onboarding.

## 1.2 Excluded

Consumer products, US market (covered by ACME Americas division), and on-premise
deployments. On-premise was excluded because the support cost per customer exceeds
the revenue threshold set in objective obj-unit-economics.

## 1.3 Boundaries

Infrastructure and platform operations are provided by the ACME Platform team
(see their scope at ../platform/01-scope.biz42.md). Data privacy and compliance
capabilities are shared with the ACME Legal & Compliance unit.

# Opportunities

<!--
biz42 chapter 5. Documents things the business could capitalize on to improve
outcomes, gain advantage, or grow. Like a risk, an opportunity is an interpretation

ISO 9001:2015 mapping: Clause 6.1 — Actions to address risks and opportunities.

For each opportunity, write a ## section with a prose paragraph describing the
opportunity, what signals or expectations it arises from, and an assessment of

Each opportunity should reference:
- The signal(s) or expectation(s) it arises from (chapters 2 and 3)
- The objective(s) that pursue it (chapter 6)
- The capabilities it may require (chapter 9)
- The products or services it may result in (chapter 10)

An opportunity without a link to at least one objective is unaddressed.
An unaddressed opportunity is not a finding — unlike risks, opportunities
can be consciously deferred. But an opportunity that has been sitting
unaddressed for multiple review cycles deserves a re-evaluation.

Order opportunities by potential value — highest first.

Example:

## Compliance as a Feature

The EU AI Act creates compliance overhead for every vendor in our market.
If we build conformity assessment tooling into the platform before competitors
do, we can turn a regulatory burden into a selling point — especially for
mid-market customers who lack the resources to build compliance processes
themselves.

Arises from: signal "EU AI Act Enforcement Timeline", expectation "Mid-Market
Customers — Time to Value".
Potential value: high (competitive differentiation, retention).
Addressed by: objective obj-compliance-feature.
Requires: capability-ml-decisioning, capability-compliance-automation (gap).
Results in: product-platform (new feature set).

## Self-Serve Onboarding

Customers expect time-to-value under 30 days, but our current onboarding
requires professional services involvement averaging 22 days. A self-serve
onboarding flow could reduce time-to-value to under 7 days, cut professional
services cost per customer by 60%, and improve the CAC/LTV ratio.

Arises from: signal "Rising Customer Acquisition Cost", expectation "Mid-Market
Customers — Time to Value".
Potential value: high (unit economics, customer satisfaction).
Addressed by: objective obj-self-serve.
Requires: capability-product-led-growth (gap).

- A deadline (by when)
- An owner (who is accountable — see chapter 8)
- Allocated resources (what is needed)

For each objective, write a ## section with a prose paragraph explaining what the

Each objective should reference:
- The risk(s) or opportunity/opportunities it addresses (chapters 4 and 5)
- The measure(s) that evaluate it (chapter 7)
- The owner accountable for it (chapter 8)
- The capabilities it requires (chapter 9)

An objective without a measure is unverifiable.
An objective without an owner is unaccountable.
An objective that addresses neither a risk nor an opportunity is unjustified.

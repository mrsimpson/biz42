# Expectations

## Knowledge workers (end users)

Knowledge workers using Assistify daily needed to find relevant past conversations and team knowledge without having to actively search — the value had to come to them implicitly through the flow of normal work.

```biz42
:::expectation
id: exp-implicit-discovery
title: Knowledge surfaces without explicit search effort
source: knowledge workers (end users)
surfaces: opp-implicit-discovery, risk-user-adoption
:::
```

### Fast, low-friction group communication

Team members expected to communicate quickly across internal and external groups — including colleagues, business partners, and customers — without the friction of email or the compliance risk of US-hosted tools.

```biz42
:::expectation
id: exp-fast-communication
title: Fast and low-friction group communication
source: knowledge workers (end users)
surfaces: opp-market-timing, risk-user-adoption
:::
```

### No new habits required

End users expected to benefit from AI without needing to learn new workflows, use explicit commands, or change how they communicate.

```biz42
:::expectation
id: exp-no-learning-curve
title: AI benefits without behavior change or learning curve
source: knowledge workers (end users)
surfaces: risk-value-communication, opp-implicit-discovery
:::
```

## Enterprise buyers / IT procurement

### GDPR and data residency compliance

Enterprise IT managers and compliance officers in DACH required that all communication data remain within EU jurisdiction, ruling out US-hosted SaaS providers. GDPR compliance and alignment with corporate data policies was a prerequisite for procurement approval.

```biz42
:::expectation
id: exp-gdpr-compliance
title: GDPR and corporate data residency compliance
source: enterprise IT procurement / compliance officers
surfaces: opp-compliance-differentiator, risk-enterprise-procurement
:::
```

### Security and access control

Enterprise buyers required encryption, SSO, and 2FA to meet internal IT security standards before approving any communication platform.

```biz42
:::expectation
id: exp-security
title: Enterprise-grade security (encryption, SSO, 2FA)
source: enterprise IT procurement
surfaces: risk-enterprise-procurement
:::
```

### Demonstrable productivity gain

Buyers needed to justify the investment to management with a measurable productivity or knowledge-sharing improvement for their teams.

```biz42
:::expectation
id: exp-roi
title: Demonstrable productivity or ROI improvement
source: enterprise buyers / management
surfaces: risk-value-communication, risk-long-sales-cycles
:::
```

### Platform reliability and availability

Enterprise customers expected the platform to be highly available and reliable — downtime in a communication tool that teams depend on daily is business-critical and directly threatens retention and trust.

```biz42
:::expectation
id: exp-reliability
title: High platform availability and reliability
source: enterprise customers (knowledge worker teams and IT procurement)
surfaces: risk-availability
:::
```

## Regulators

### GDPR conformity

EU data protection regulation (GDPR / DSGVO) required that any platform handling employee and customer communication data process and store that data lawfully, with appropriate consent and data residency controls.

```biz42
:::expectation

source: EU / DACH regulatory authorities
surfaces: opp-compliance-differentiator, risk-enterprise-procurement
:::
```

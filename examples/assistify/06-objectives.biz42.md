# Objectives

## Establish Assistify as the GDPR-compliant team chat of choice in DACH

Assistify committed to winning enterprise customers in DACH by leading with its compliance advantage. The objective was to convert the legal and regulatory barrier that blocked Slack into a direct sales driver, closing at least five enterprise contracts within 12 months of product launch.

```biz42
:::objective
id: obj-enterprise-sales
title: Win 5 enterprise contracts in DACH within 12 months by leading with compliance
addresses: risk-enterprise-procurement, risk-long-sales-cycles, opp-compliance-differentiator, opp-market-timing
measured-by: measure-enterprise-contracts, measure-sales-cycle, measure-compliance-rate
owner: owner-ceo
requires: cap-enterprise-sales, cap-gdpr-infrastructure, cap-translation
:::
```

## Make the implicit AI knowledge discovery value tangible to buyers and users

Assistify committed to closing the gap between what the product did and what buyers and users could perceive. This meant building demo scenarios with real data, developing reference cases, and ensuring every new user experienced a knowledge discovery result within their first week.

```biz42
:::objective
id: obj-vp-clarity
title: Make implicit AI value tangible — measurable activation within first week for new users
addresses: risk-value-communication, risk-user-adoption, opp-implicit-discovery
measured-by: measure-discovery-activation, measure-nps
owner: owner-cto
requires: cap-ai-knowledge-discovery, cap-onboarding
:::
```

## Differentiate on AI to build a moat against commoditisation

Assistify committed to deepening its AI knowledge discovery capabilities to stay ahead of the commoditisation pressure from dominant chat platforms. The goal was to make the AI layer so valuable that switching cost exceeded any network advantage of Slack.

## Ensure enterprise-grade availability through Rocket.Chat dedicated hosting

A small team cannot operate enterprise-grade infrastructure independently. Assistify committed to meeting customer uptime expectations by procuring dedicated hosting from Rocket.Chat, accepting the administrative overhead of vendor management as the price of reliability. The goal was to eliminate availability as a procurement objection or churn driver.

```biz42
:::objective
id: obj-availability
title: Ensure enterprise-grade platform availability via Rocket.Chat dedicated hosting
addresses: risk-availability
measured-by: measure-uptime
owner: owner-cto
requires: cap-rocketchat-hosting, cap-gdpr-infrastructure
:::
```

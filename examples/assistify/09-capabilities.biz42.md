## Rocket.Chat dedicated hosting relationship

Assistify compensated for the small team's inability to operate enterprise-grade infrastructure by procuring dedicated hosting from Rocket.Chat, the open-source project on which Assistify was built. This provided the reliability and uptime enterprise customers required, but introduced substantial administrative overhead in managing the vendor relationship, procurement cycles, and contract renewals.

```biz42
:::capability
id: cap-rocketchat-hosting

status: exists
enables: prod-group-chat-platform, prod-livechat, prod-translation, prod-chatops, prod-knowledge-management
owner: owner-cto
:::
```

# Products and Services

## Group chat platform (core product)

Assistify's primary product was a GDPR-compliant group chat platform for enterprise knowledge worker teams. It enabled internal and external collaboration, integrated with wikis and SharePoint, and embedded the implicit AI knowledge discovery layer that surfaced relevant past conversations without user effort.

```biz42
:::product
id: prod-group-chat-platform
title: GDPR-compliant group chat platform with implicit AI knowledge discovery
fulfills: exp-implicit-discovery, exp-fast-communication, exp-no-learning-curve, exp-gdpr-compliance, exp-security
owner: owner-cto
:::
```

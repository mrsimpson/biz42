### Dependency on Rocket.Chat as open-source foundation

Assistify was built on top of Rocket.Chat, an open-source group chat platform. This provided a solid technical foundation but introduced a structural dependency: reliable, enterprise-grade hosting had to be procured from Rocket.Chat directly, as self-hosting at that quality level was not feasible for a small team.

:::

# Evaluation

## Monthly sales pipeline review

The founding CEO reviewed the enterprise sales pipeline monthly to track deal progress, assess sales cycle duration, and identify prospects stuck in procurement. This informed prioritisation of sales effort and compliance documentation support.

```biz42
:::evaluation
id: eval-sales-review
title: Monthly enterprise sales pipeline review
method: Monthly review by CEO — deal stage, cycle duration, compliance blockers
evaluates: measure-enterprise-contracts, measure-sales-cycle, measure-compliance-rate
:::
```

## Quarterly user adoption review

The founding CTO reviewed daily active user data per deployed customer team quarterly to identify teams at risk of low adoption or churn, and to assess whether knowledge discovery activation was occurring within the first week.

```biz42
:::evaluation
id: eval-adoption-review
title: Quarterly user adoption and knowledge discovery activation review
method: Quarterly analysis of DAU data and discovery activation logs per customer team
evaluates: measure-dau, measure-discovery-activation
:::
```

## Customer NPS survey

Assistify ran a periodic Net Promoter Score survey with active users in deployed teams to gauge satisfaction and identify advocates for word-of-mouth referrals within large accounts.

```biz42
:::evaluation
id: eval-nps-survey
title: Periodic NPS survey with active users in deployed teams
method: Periodic in-app survey, reviewed by CEO and CTO; results shared with enterprise contacts
evaluates: measure-nps
:::
```

# Opportunities

## Market opportunities

### Compliance differentiator vs US-hosted competitors

GDPR and corporate data sovereignty requirements in DACH effectively barred US-hosted platforms like Slack from many enterprise procurement processes. Assistify, as a GDPR-compliant, EU-hosted alternative, had a structural compliance advantage that opened doors that were closed to competitors.

### Market timing — group chat becoming mainstream

The rapid rise of team chat as the primary workplace communication layer meant the market was actively looking for solutions. Assistify could ride this wave while positioning the compliance and AI angle as the reason to choose it over Slack.

incidents, this creates an internal mandate — the organization needs to act,

## Location-Based Alert Routing

No existing solution can answer "who is the nearest person who can help?"
Proximity-based routing fundamentally changes response time — instead of
alerting 200 people and hoping one is nearby, alert the 3 people who are
actually on the same floor.

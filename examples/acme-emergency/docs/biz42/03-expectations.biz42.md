# Expectations

## Employees — Fast Help When It Matters

Employees who trigger an alert expect a qualified person to arrive within
minutes. They expect the system to be simple enough to use under stress, and
that it won't be used to surveil them.

```biz42
:::expectation
id: exp-employees-fast-help
title: Employees — Fast Help When It Matters
source: employees
surfaces: risk-adoption, risk-works-council
:::
```

## Safety Officers — Compliance and Audit Trail

Safety officers need to demonstrate systematic alerting capability and expect
the system to produce records of alert issuance, response times, and outcomes
for compliance with ArbSchG and DGUV regulations.

```biz42
:::expectation
id: exp-safety-compliance
title: Safety Officers — Compliance and Audit Trail
source: internal safety and compliance
surfaces: opp-internal-mandate
:::
```

## Works Council — Privacy Safeguards

The works council expects clear boundaries: location data used only for active
alert situations, no continuous tracking, no performance monitoring derived from
location data, transparent data retention, and genuinely voluntary participation.

```biz42
:::expectation
id: exp-works-council-privacy
title: Works Council — Privacy Safeguards
source: works council (Betriebsrat)
surfaces: risk-works-council
:::
```

## Management — Cost-Effective Alternative

Management sponsors expect a solution that costs significantly less per site
than the existing enterprise notification system, with company-wide rollout
within 12 months.

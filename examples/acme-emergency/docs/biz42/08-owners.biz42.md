# Owners

## Product Lead

Accountable for product-market fit (internal market), works council
negotiation, adoption strategy, and the overall alerting service definition.
The works council relationship is critical and requires a dedicated owner
who understands both the product and the labor relations dimension.

Owns objectives: obj-privacy-architecture, obj-adoption-rate.
Maintains capabilities: (delegates to tech lead).
Delivers: product-alert-service (product direction).

## Tech Lead

Accountable for the technical platform: alert delivery reliability,
location-based routing, offline resilience, moving-asset support, and
sensor integration. Makes architecture decisions that must preserve the
option for future spin-out (see opportunity-spin-out).

Owns objectives: obj-location-routing, obj-moving-assets, obj-offline-resilience,
obj-sensor-bridge.
Maintains capabilities: capability-alert-delivery, capability-location-routing,
capability-sensor-ingestion, capability-asset-integration.
Delivers: product-alert-service (technical delivery), product-sensor-bridge.

## Project Lead

Accountable for the company-wide rollout: site-by-site deployment coordination,
infrastructure readiness, stakeholder management across sites, and the
12-month timeline. This is a program management role, not a product role.

Owns objectives: obj-time-to-deployment.
Maintains capabilities: capability-platform-integration.
Delivers: (rollout execution, not product delivery).
